Measurz

Google Play
Android Native · Kotlin / Java

## Measurz – Functional Performance & Capacity Assessment Platform **Measurz** is a professional client assessment application designed to help health, fitness, rehabilitation, and performance professionals efficiently measure and track their clients' physical capabilities, movement, strength, flexibility, endurance, and performance. The application digitises the complete assessment workflow, allowing professionals to perform assessments directly from a mobile device while maintaining engagement and communication with their clients. It provides access to **500+ assessment tests**, 30+ pre-built testing templates, custom templates, favourites, reassessments, and a range of built-in assessment tools. ### Key Features * **500+ Functional Assessment Tests** covering strength, flexibility, mobility, balance, endurance, power, speed, agility, and overall physical performance. * **30+ Pre-built Assessment Templates** to help professionals quickly start assessments. * **Custom Testing Templates** allowing users to create and share standardised assessment templates across their teams. * **Assessment Favourites** for quick access to frequently used testing templates. * **Quick Reassessment** allowing professionals to repeat previously performed tests efficiently. * **Inclinometer** for measuring joint angles during range-of-motion and flexibility assessments. * **AI-Driven Video Analysis** to automatically analyse movement and calculate body/joint angles during exercises and assessments. * **Countdown Timer & Rep Counter** for strength, endurance, power, and speed testing. * **Stopwatch** for accurately measuring balance, endurance, agility, speed, and fitness tests. * **Rep Counter** to help professionals focus on their clients without manually tracking repetitions. * Integration with MAT assessment tools including **MAT, MegaMAT, Hop MAT, and Muscle Meter**. ### BLE Device Integration The application integrates with multiple **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)** measurement devices, including: * **Muscle Meter** * **Gripper** * **Anker** These devices capture real-time force/strength measurements while a client or sportsperson performs a test or applies force during rehabilitation and performance assessments. The BLE integration enables the application to: * Discover and connect to compatible BLE devices. * Receive real-time measurement data from connected devices. * Process and display live force readings during assessments. * Monitor client performance during strength and rehabilitation exercises. * Visualise live BLE measurements through interactive real-time graphs. ### Real-Time Data Visualisation **SciChart** is integrated into the application to display real-time BLE measurement data in interactive graphs. Force readings received from connected devices are plotted live, allowing professionals to monitor performance and analyse force output during an assessment. ### Subscription & In-App Purchase The application uses **RevenueCat** to manage subscription-based access and in-app purchases. Subscription plans include: * **Solo Plan** – $219.99/year – 1 user * **Small Team** – $400/year – 2–4 users * **Medium Team** – $800/year – 5–9 users * **Larger Teams** – Custom pricing – 10+ users * **Free Plan** – Limited access for testing Paid plans provide a 30-day free trial, unlimited clients, and unlimited assessment sessions, with subscriptions automatically renewing unless cancelled before the renewal period. ### Impact Measurz replaces traditional manual assessment workflows with a digital, data-driven assessment platform. By combining **functional testing, BLE hardware integration, AI-powered movement analysis, real-time data visualisation, and subscription management**, the application helps professionals perform assessments faster, capture more accurate data, and track client performance effectively.
